Mary Riley Styles Library

Context-the Mary Riley Styles Public Library was founded in 1899. Its online catalog was launched in 1990. It serves Northern Virginia, Washington, D.C. and adjacent parts of Maryland. Their mission is supporting community; inspiring discovery; and promoting literacy. Their vision is a sustainable, informed, and caring community of lifelong learners. Users: The Mary Riley Styles library hosts preschool storytimes for kids ages to 2-4; STEAM-based crafts and activities for kids grades K-5; and book club to-go kits, genealogical services, and board game Sundays for teens and adults. Content: The Mary Riley Stiles Library offers print books, audiobooks, ebooks, and magazines for 21, days all of which are renewable and reservable; DVDs for 7 days which are renewable and reservable; and Lucky Day books for 14 days which are reservable but not renewable. Unique Features and pros: The catalog features a very nice mixture of print books, ebooks, audiobooks, print magazines and e-magazines, as well as an abundance of Spanish-language materials. Cons and suggestions for improvement: The resources and events for teens were lumped in with the events for adults, so it could use more resources and events aimed explicitly at teen.
